COMPETITOR BRIEFING — Multi-Source Conflict & Media Analytics
=============================================================

Challenge
---------
Analyse organised violence (UCDP) alongside media-coded geopolitics signals (GDELT)
for: Afghanistan, Pakistan, United States, Russia, Ukraine, Iran, Israel.

Datasets provided
-----------------
1) war_terror_violence_200k.csv
   - UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset subset
   - Organised violence events with fatalities (war / one-sided / non-state)
   - Time span: mainly 1989-2025

2) gdelt_focus_feb_apr_2026.csv
   - GDELT media-coded events for the same focus countries
   - Time span: February-April 2026

3) country_month_panel.csv  (RECOMMENDED analysis table)
   - Pre-joined on country + year_month
   - Join type used: FULL OUTER style union of keys (coverage_flag shows source)

4) country_summary_panel.csv
   - Country-level LEFT/FULL style summary (easy starter table)

CRITICAL dataset RULES (read carefully)
-------------------------------
1. UCDP and GDELT are DIFFERENT sources. One GDELT row is NOT one UCDP incident.
2. Do NOT join on event IDs.
3. joins:
   - country + year_month  (panel already provided)
   - country-level aggregates
4. GDELT is news volume / narrative signal; UCDP is conflict fatality ground truth.
5. Time overlap is limited: UCDP historical vs GDELT Feb-Apr 2026.
   Use GDELT for near-term narrative heat; UCDP for long-run conflict baseline.
6. United States will dominate GDELT counts due to media coverage bias.

Join types explained
--------------------
- FULL OUTER JOIN (country, year_month): keep months present in either source.
  Implemented in country_month_panel via coverage_flag =
    both | ucdp_only | gdelt_only
- Country-level join: always matched for the 7 focus countries in country_summary_panel.




Additional source added
-----------------------
5) middle east type of conflict.xlsx (+ derived middle_east_conflict_monthly.csv)
   - ACLED-style weekly Middle East political violence aggregates
   - Joined on country + year_month into country_month_panel_with_middle_east.csv
   - Best overlap with focus set: Iran, Israel; also broader ME context

Use country_month_panel_with_middle_east.csv as the fullest joined table.
